Janggi - Big Text AI Game & Review: Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

Bareun App Studio ("we") publishes the Android app "Janggi - Big Text AI Game & Review" ("the app"). This is a short summary of how the app handles information. The Korean version (https://minimaljjang.github.io/janggi-app-public/privacy_ko.html) is the primary text.

Summary

  • The app has no account, sign-up or login. It does not ask for your name, phone number or email address.
  • Your game records, review results and settings are stored only on your phone. We do not run a server that receives them.
  • The computer opponent (open-source Fairy-Stockfish engine) and the review analysis run on your device and work offline.
  • Ads are shown through Google AdMob and the optional one-time purchase "Premium" (no ads, more review scenes and deeper analysis) is handled by Google Play Billing. Google processes some data in those services (see below).
  • We do not sell your data.

Data stored on your device only

Saved games (the unfinished game and the last 30 finished games), review analysis results, promotion progress, and settings (text size, sound, voice) plus a cached flag whether Premium is owned. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes all of it. If you use Android backup, the operating system may include app data in your Google account backup; we have no access to it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows one kind of ad: a full-screen ad when you start a new game (never during a game). Premium owners see no ads and the ad SDK is not started for them. Google may collect and process, automatically and for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention: the Android advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device and app information, ad and app interactions, and diagnostics. Data is encrypted in transit. In regions where consent is required (EEA, UK, etc.) a Google consent form (UMP) is shown first and no ads are requested before consent; a "Privacy options" entry appears in Settings where applicable. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads). See https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Payment details are handled entirely by Google Play; the app and the developer never receive card or account details. The app only checks whether Premium is owned.

Voice

The calls "장군 / 멍군 / 외통수 / 빅장" are pre-recorded audio files created with AI speech synthesis and played offline. The optional "read the review aloud" feature uses your phone's built-in text-to-speech; the app does not send the text anywhere. The app does not use the microphone, camera, contacts, location or photos.

What we do not do

We run no analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own, and we do not share or sell personal information. Data is processed by Google only when you see an ad or make a purchase.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 14 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Your choices and contact

You can delete all app data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. We hold no information that identifies you. Google-held data can be managed at https://myaccount.google.com. Questions: ecosketch21@gmail.com

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the app; changes apply from the new effective date.
